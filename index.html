Disc-yphus

Physics-based "Foddian" climber · PC · Coming soon

Scoring par is out of the picture, you will be lucky to see the back 9. Launch, steer, and flop your way up 5 unique areas with ridiculous obstacles to find 18 sets of chains. Pilot your disc from a quiet meadow to the edge of space. Every fall is real, but don't be disc-couraged.
